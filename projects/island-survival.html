Island Survival

My first Unreal Engine 5 project, built solo while following an online course. The goal was the foundation of a survival game: building, inventory and a health loop, and learning how to structure gameplay logic in Blueprints.

Type
Online course project
Engine
Unreal Engine 5
Language
Blueprints
Role
Gameplay programmer (solo)
01

Modular building

Players place walls, floors, ceilings, windows and doors on a grid or free-form, with rotation and snapping. Traces check placement in real time and a ghost mesh shows whether a piece is valid. The system respects collision and terrain slope, and enforces structural order: no roof without walls.

In-game
02

Inventory

Items can be picked up, stored and managed in slots that update dynamically, with an inventory UI that opens and closes over gameplay.

In-game
03

Health & damage

Tracks current and max health, applies damage from enemies or the environment, supports healing, updates the UI and triggers a death state at zero. It was designed so armour or other stats could be added later without changing the core.

In-game

What I learned

Planning systems like the inventory and building before implementing them saved a lot of rework. Small touches like placement feedback made a big difference to how the game felt. Working solo meant covering programming, design and debugging, and taught me to break big systems into small parts I could test one at a time.
