About

Hi, I'm Cris. I've wanted to make games since I was a kid, and these days I focus on the programming side: the systems that make a game feel good to play. I like taking a mechanic like blocking or lock-on, working out exactly how it should behave, and building it as a clean, reusable component.

My Computer Science degree covered AI, human-computer interaction and offensive security, so I care about performance and systems, and I don't just stop once something works. I'm now at Breda University of Applied Sciences doing Creative Media & Game Technologies on the programming track, where I write C++ without an engine as well as in Unreal.

I also make tutorial videos and like helping other people learn. If you want to talk about gameplay programming, get in touch.
